How to get an accurate reading
Keep your eyes open and fixed on one point. Alpha rises sharply when the eyes close — far more than any emotion moves it — so a single blink-heavy stretch will swamp the measurement. (Sisode 2016 states the opposite, that alpha is stronger with eyes open; that is an error in the paper and following it will spoil your data.)

Sit still through the baseline. The first 12 seconds after connecting measure your own resting band powers. Every reading afterwards is scored against that, per Sisode's baseline-first method. Jaw clenching or eyebrow movement during this window will bias everything that follows — press Recalibrate if it happens.

The rules below are engineering, not published findings. Neither attached paper contains a threshold rule set. Every one of the 13 methods surveyed by Li, Chao & Zhang trains a supervised classifier on labelled data, reaching 62–93.5% accuracy. The mapping here is inferred from the descriptive band associations in those papers and should be described that way.
